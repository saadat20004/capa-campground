The place

Less campground. More homestead.

Capa sits in the lush Quinault River Valley, between National Forest land and Olympic National Park, near the wild Colonel Bob Wilderness. Towering evergreens, green lawns and a small off grid farm give it a calm, timeless feel.

It's simple and relaxed: just six spacious sites, a garden, a flock of chickens, and hosts Paul & Carol who walk you round when you arrive. At the end of a trail day you come back to real stillness.
